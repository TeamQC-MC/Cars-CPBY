GENERAL CONTROLS
D-PAD UP OR 2 - SHIFTS GEARS UP TO ACCELERATE LIGHTNING, OR STOP WHEN REVERSING.
D-PAD DOWN OR 8 - SHIFTS GEARS DOWN TO SLOW LIGHTNING DOWN OR SHIFT INTO REVERSE. HOLD WHILE GOING FORWARD TO STOP.
D-PAD LEFT OR 4 - TURNS LIGHTNING TO HIS LEFT. HOLD TO CONTINUE TURNING.
D-PAD RIGHT OR 6 - TURNS LIGHTNING TO HIS RIGHT. HOLD TO CONTINUE TURNING.
ACTION KEY OR 5 - TALK TO A NEARBY CHARACTER. A SPEECH BUBBLE ICON APPEARS WHEN LIGHTNING IS CLOSE ENOUGH TO TALK.
SPECIAL MANEUVERS
ACTION KEY OR 5 - ACTIVATES A TURBO BOOST, WHEN AVAILABLE. THE TURBO BOOST CAN BE USED TO QUICKLY BLOW BY YOUR OPPONENTS DURING RACES, OR JUST GET A TEMPORARY BOOST IN SPEED.
ACTION KEY OR 5, WHILE DRIVING BACKWARDS - PERFORMS A REVERSAL: LIGHTNING NOW CONTINUES IN THE SAME DIRECTION BUT FACES FORWARDS. THIS IS A QUICK AND CONVENIENT WAY OF CHANGING DIRECTION.
MENUS ETC.
ACTION KEY OR 5 - SELECT ITEM FROM MENU.
D-PAD LEFT OR 4, RIGHT OR 6 - TOGGLE SOUND ON OR OFF FROM MENU. ON THE HIGH SCORE SCREEN, SWITCH TO VIEW SCORES FOR ANOTHER NPC'S MISSIONS.
D-PAD UP OR 2, DOWN OR 8 - SCROLL MENUS AND TEXT DIALOGS.
IN THE MENUS AND DIALOGS, PRESS THE LEFT SOFTKEY TO SELECT OR ACCEPT, AND THE RIGHT SOFTKEY TO CANCEL OR RETURN TO THE PREVIOUS SCREEN.
IN GAME, PRESS THE RIGHT SOFT KEY - INDICATED BY THE SMALL TRIANGLE ICON - TO ACCESS THE IN-GAME MENU.
